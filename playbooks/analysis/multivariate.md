---
id: multivariate
title: Multivariate structure
applies_when:
  - has_type: numeric
    min_count: 3
tags: [multivariate, pca, clustering]
templates: [pca_overview, kmeans_explore]
---
Use for exploratory analysis of many numeric columns to find hidden structure. PCA helps visualize the overall variance, while K-means helps identify potential clusters. Best when there are 3 or more numeric columns.
